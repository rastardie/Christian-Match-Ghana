# Browse View Filtering - User Guide

## Overview

The Browse view now includes powerful filtering tools to help you find the perfect match based on your faith, values, and preferences.

## Quick Start

### Accessing Filters

1. Navigate to the **Browse** view from the bottom navigation
2. Look for the **Filters** button in the top-right corner
3. Tap the button to open the filter panel

### Understanding the Filter Badge

- The orange badge on the Filters button shows how many filters are currently active
- Example: A badge showing "5" means you have 5 active filters applied

## Filter Categories

### 1. Denomination

**What it does:** Shows only profiles from specific Christian denominations

**Options Available:**

- Pentecostal
- Charismatic
- Methodist
- Presbyterian
- Catholic
- Baptist
- Anglican
- Adventist
- Evangelical
- Non-Denominational
- Other

**How to use:**

- Tap one or more denominations to include them in your search
- Selected options will be highlighted in orange
- Tap again to deselect

**Example:** Select "Pentecostal" and "Methodist" to see profiles from either denomination

---

### 2. Church Attendance

**What it does:** Filters profiles by how often they attend church

**Options Available:**

- Weekly
- Multiple times a week
- Irregular
- Monthly

**How to use:**

- Choose the attendance frequency that matters to you
- Multiple selections work as "OR" - profiles matching any selected option will show

**Example:** Select "Weekly" and "Multiple times a week" to find highly active church members

---

### 3. Relationship Intentions

**What it does:** Shows profiles based on what they're looking for

**Options Available:**

- Courtship - Traditional dating leading to marriage
- Marriage Soon - Ready for immediate commitment
- Friendship - Building connections first

**How to use:**

- Select the intention(s) that align with your goals
- This ensures you match with people on the same page

**Example:** Select "Marriage Soon" if you're ready for a serious commitment

---

### 4. Faith Maturity

**What it does:** Filters by spiritual growth and development

**Options Available:**

- Growing - Developing in faith
- Mature - Established faith walk
- Leader - Active in ministry/leadership

**How to use:**

- Choose the level of spiritual maturity you prefer
- Helps find someone at a compatible faith stage

**Example:** Select "Mature" and "Leader" for spiritually grounded partners

---

### 5. Age Range

**What it does:** Sets minimum and maximum age limits

**How to use:**

1. Drag the top slider to set minimum age (18-60)
2. Drag the bottom slider to set maximum age (18-60)
3. Current range is displayed above the sliders

**Tips:**

- Default range is 18-60 (shows all ages)
- Sliders automatically prevent min from exceeding max
- Adjust based on your preference and life stage

**Example:** Set range to 25-35 for peers in similar life stage

---

### 6. Maximum Distance

**What it does:** Limits how far away matches can be located

**How to use:**

- Drag the slider from 10 km to 10,000 km
- 10,000 km essentially means "any distance"
- Distance is calculated from profiles' location data

**Tips:**

- Start with a smaller radius if you prefer nearby matches
- Increase if you're open to long-distance relationships
- "Any" distance is good if you're willing to relocate

**Example:** Set to 50 km to find local matches you can meet easily

---

### 7. Lifestyle Preferences

**What it does:** Filters based on lifestyle habits

**Options Available:**

- ✓ No Drinking - Excludes profiles who drink alcohol
- ✓ No Smoking - Excludes profiles who smoke

**How to use:**

- Check the box(es) for dealbreakers
- Only profiles meeting these criteria will show

**Example:** Check both if you want a completely substance-free partner

---

### 8. Verification

**What it does:** Shows only verified profiles

**Option:**

- ✓ Verified Profiles Only

**How to use:**

- Check this box to see only identity-verified users
- Verified profiles have a blue checkmark badge

**Benefits:**

- Increased trust and safety
- Higher quality matches
- Reduced risk of fake profiles

## Using Filters Effectively

### Strategy Tips

#### 1. Start Broad, Then Narrow

- Begin with 1-2 essential filters (e.g., denomination, intentions)
- Add more filters if you have too many results
- Remove filters if you have too few results

#### 2. Prioritize Your Dealbreakers

- Start with absolute must-haves
- Add nice-to-haves later
- Remember: More filters = fewer matches

#### 3. Balance Quality and Quantity

- Too many filters might exclude great matches
- Too few filters means browsing many incompatible profiles
- Find your sweet spot

### Common Filter Combinations

#### The Local Believer

- Denomination: Your preference
- Church Attendance: Weekly/Multiple times a week
- Maximum Distance: 50 km
- No Drinking: Yes
- No Smoking: Yes

#### The Serious Seeker

- Relationship Intentions: Marriage Soon
- Faith Maturity: Mature/Leader
- Verified Profiles Only: Yes
- Age Range: Narrow to your preference

#### The Open Explorer

- Denomination: Multiple selections
- Maximum Distance: Any
- Relationship Intentions: Courtship/Friendship
- Age Range: Wide (e.g., 22-40)

## Managing Your Filters

### Applying Filters

1. Select all your desired criteria
2. Tap **Apply Filters** at the bottom
3. The filter panel closes
4. Browse updated results

### Clearing Filters

- **Clear All** button resets everything to defaults
- Filters button badge will show "0"
- All profiles become visible again

### Viewing Results

- Profile count updates in real-time as you select filters
- "12 profiles found" shows how many matches you'll see
- If count is zero, consider removing some filters

## Troubleshooting

### No Profiles Found

**Problem:** Filter panel shows "0 profiles found"

**Solutions:**

1. Tap **Clear All** to reset filters
2. Remove some filter criteria one at a time
3. Expand age range or distance
4. Deselect lifestyle filters temporarily

### Too Many Results

**Problem:** Browsing becomes overwhelming

**Solutions:**

1. Add more specific filters (intentions, faith maturity)
2. Narrow age range
3. Reduce maximum distance
4. Add lifestyle preferences
5. Enable verified only

### Filter Not Working

**Problem:** Results don't match selected filters

**Solutions:**

1. Close and reopen filter panel
2. Tap **Apply Filters** button
3. Check that filters are actually selected (orange highlight)
4. Refresh the app if issue persists

## FAQs

**Q: How many filters should I use?**
A: Start with 2-3 key filters. Too many reduces matches; too few wastes time.

**Q: Do filters affect who sees my profile?**
A: No, filters only affect who YOU see. Others may still see and connect with you.

**Q: Can I save filter combinations?**
A: Not currently, but this feature is planned for a future update.

**Q: Are verified profiles better?**
A: Verified profiles have confirmed identities, adding trust and authenticity.

**Q: What if my perfect match doesn't meet all my filters?**
A: That's why we recommend starting with essential filters only. You might miss great matches with too many restrictions.

**Q: How often do filter options update?**
A: Filter options are based on available profile data and update with each new member.

**Q: Can I filter by multiple denominations?**
A: Yes! Select as many as you like. Profiles matching ANY selected denomination will show.
